# Service Level Agreement (SLA)

## Project

**Product:** MTP Digital Agriculture & Nutrition Grade 9 Interactive Digital Content  
**Subject/Lot:** Grade 9 Agriculture  
**Provider:** Mountain Publishers / MTP Digital  
**Client:** Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development (KICD) / Procuring Entity  
**SLA Term:** Three (3) years from commissioning, with post-warranty support as agreed in the contract.

## 1. Purpose

This Service Level Agreement defines the support, maintenance, response time, update, and escalation commitments for the MTP Digital Grade 9 Agriculture & Nutrition interactive HTML5 content package.

## 2. Supported Content

The supported content includes:

- HTML5 interactive learning package.
- Main `index.html` and all referenced JavaScript, CSS, images, PDF, icons, and local assets.
- Offline/PWA functionality.
- Interactive lessons, quizzes, games, glossary, notes, teacher dashboard, progress tools, and report features.
- Bug fixes, compatibility updates, and approved content corrections.

## 3. Service Provision

The provider will maintain the digital content during the SLA period by:

- Providing updates, releases, and corrected versions at no additional cost during the warranty period.
- Fixing functional defects that prevent learners, teachers, or reviewers from using the content.
- Maintaining compatibility with common browsers including Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari where technically feasible.
- Supporting deployment in offline, online, wired, wireless, and mixed school ICT environments.
- Providing guidance for installation, packaging, and use of the HTML5 content package.

## 4. Support Availability

Support shall be available through the following channels:

- Email support.
- Phone support.
- Chat or messaging support, where available.
- Remote troubleshooting, where connectivity permits.

Standard business-hour support will be available Monday to Friday, excluding official public holidays. Critical content-access issues will be acknowledged on a 24/7 basis during the active contract lifecycle.

## 5. Response And Resolution Times

| Priority | Description | Acknowledgement Target | Resolution / Workaround Target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Critical | Content cannot open, major offline failure, package unusable, or blocker affecting many users. | Within 2 hours | Within 2 hours where feasible, or provide a workaround and fix plan within 1 business day. |
| High | Major feature failure affecting quizzes, games, PDF access, progress saving, or teacher tools. | Within 4 business hours | Within 2 business days. |
| Medium | Non-blocking defect, display issue, minor browser-specific issue, or limited content correction. | Within 1 business day | Within 5 business days. |
| Low | Cosmetic issue, wording improvement, documentation update, or enhancement request. | Within 2 business days | Scheduled for the next planned release where approved. |

Resolution timelines may depend on issue complexity, availability of approval for curriculum/content changes, and access to affected devices or environments.

## 6. Updates And Releases

The provider will issue updates when required for:

- Approved curriculum/content corrections.
- Browser compatibility fixes.
- Security or packaging improvements.
- Accessibility and usability improvements.
- Bug fixes reported during review, commissioning, or live use.

Each update will include a brief release note describing the change, date, affected files, and version.

## 7. Escalation Procedure

If an issue is not resolved within the stated response or resolution target, it will be escalated as follows:

1. **Level 1 - Support Desk:** Logs the issue, confirms reproduction steps, and provides first response.
2. **Level 2 - Technical Lead:** Investigates code, packaging, browser, device, or offline deployment issues.
3. **Level 3 - Project Manager:** Coordinates timelines, client communication, release approval, and delivery.
4. **Level 4 - Management Review:** Handles unresolved critical issues, contractual concerns, or repeated SLA breaches.

Escalated issues will include an updated status, responsible person, expected next action, and estimated fix or workaround time.

## 8. Maintenance Scope

Maintenance includes:

- Defect correction.
- Content package rebuilding.
- Browser and device compatibility checks.
- Offline-cache and asset-reference corrections.
- Documentation updates.
- Packaging support for submission and deployment.

Maintenance excludes:

- New curriculum content not approved under the contract.
- Third-party platform failures outside the provider's control.
- Device hardware faults, operating system corruption, or network infrastructure failure.
- Unauthorized modification of the content package by third parties.

## 9. Security And Data Handling

The app stores learner progress locally in the browser using offline storage. The provider will:

- Avoid unnecessary collection of personal data.
- Not require a server database for standard package use.
- Support secure packaging and distribution practices.
- Correct any identified client-side security issue that affects content use or learner data integrity.

## 10. Warranty And Post-Warranty Support

The provider commits to full support during the three-year warranty period. After the warranty period, the provider will provide support and maintenance for two (2) additional years where required by the contract or a mutually agreed support arrangement.

## 11. Reporting

For each reported issue, the provider will maintain a record containing:

- Date and time reported.
- Reporter/contact.
- Priority level.
- Description and reproduction steps.
- Status.
- Resolution or workaround.
- Date closed.

## 12. Review

This SLA may be reviewed periodically by the provider and client to reflect approved contract changes, new deployment environments, or updated support requirements.
