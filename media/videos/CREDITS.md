# Offline video credits

These lesson videos are local, lower-resolution transcodes of openly reusable media hosted by Wikimedia Commons. No editorial changes were made beyond Wikimedia's format/resolution transcoding. English captions and written transcripts for every video are defined in `scripts/app.js` (`LESSON_VIDEOS`) and shown in the Media tab.
